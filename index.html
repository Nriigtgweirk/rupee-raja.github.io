The Urban Middle-Class Inflation Index (UMC-CPI)

Official CPI measures (such as India's CPI-Urban) fail urban middle-class households earning ₹80,000 to ₹2,500,000+ monthly in Tier-1 and Tier-2 cities. By allocating over 36.8% to raw staples while under-representing modern expense drivers like private education, health insurance, gated society maintenance, and digital lifestyle services, official prints severely understate real purchasing power erosion.
